Business-rule notes
1H applies to Smartphones received by Carry In / Online; without inventory data, this is a turnaround measure, not proof that parts were in stock. 2H PO is measured for Smartphone jobs marked Is Shortage=Yes, using the first valid PO/AP date. The 3-day view measures PO date to repair completion and is provisional until management confirms its exact start point. Returned, Repair Completed, SWAP Certification and Non Repairable are treated as terminal statuses; SWAP Approved remains open. Repair duration is calculated from Created On to Repair Complete Date. “207” is treated as an invalid date value, not a timestamp. Bounce is an in-report repeat-device indicator using prior Return On to the next Created On within 30 days; earlier history outside uploaded reports may be missing. CRM Total Amt and Accumulated Received Amt are shown separately.
